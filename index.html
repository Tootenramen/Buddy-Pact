    A shared vocabulary

    Signals: say it in one word

    When talking is hard, a colour is easy. Copy a message, send it, done.

    Green

    All good

    Reply with anything small. That is the whole job.

    Amber

    Stretched thin

    Offer a time in the next day or two. Ten minutes is plenty.

    Red

    Need you today

    Drop what you can and call. Listen first. Do not try to fix it.

    Red means: call a real person. If someone may be in danger or you are thinking about hurting yourself, do not wait for your buddy. Use the Help panel for crisis lines and emergency options.

    Where people meet

    Local meetupsStay for the chat after the talk. The hallway is where people find each other.
    Conference hallwaysSay hello to someone who looks as tired as you. Swap contact details, not business cards.
    Online communitiesLook for people who answer kindly more than once. A long thread beats a quick reply.
    Ex-colleague groupsOld teammates already know your language. Message the one you miss.
